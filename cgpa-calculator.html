FAQ

CGPA calculator: frequently asked questions.

How is VU CGPA calculated?

Virtual University calculates your official CGPA using its own relative grading system, which weighs your result against class performance for each course and semester. This process is not publicly documented in detail and can vary. This calculator instead uses the standard, widely used 4.0 GPA scale, credit-hour weighted, to give you a helpful estimate for personal planning.

Is this calculator official or exact?

No. This tool is not affiliated with Virtual University and does not reproduce VU's exact grading formula. It is an estimation tool for personal planning only. Your official CGPA is calculated by VU and appears on your VULMS transcript, which should always be treated as the authoritative record.

What GPA do I need to avoid academic probation?

Universities generally expect students to maintain a minimum CGPA to remain in good academic standing, and falling below that threshold for consecutive semesters can lead to a warning or probation. Exact thresholds and policies are set by VU and can vary by program, so always confirm the current policy on your VULMS portal or with your academic advisor rather than relying on a general figure.

How can I improve my CGPA?

Focus on consistent quiz, assignment and GDB scores since they add up over the semester, prepare early for midterm and finalterm exams with organized notes, and use this calculator each semester to see how upcoming grades will move your cumulative average. Structured exam preparation files and LMS support can also help you stay on track.
