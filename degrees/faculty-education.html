About This Faculty

Faculty of Education

The Faculty of Education at Virtual University offers the B.Ed. (Hons) Elementary and B.Ed. (Hons.) Early Childhood Care and Education programs, preparing graduates for teaching careers with a shared foundation in child development and pedagogy.

FAQ

Common questions about this faculty.

Which degrees fall under Faculty of Education?

B.Ed. (Hons) Elementary and B.Ed. (Hons.) Early Childhood Care and Education both fall under this faculty.

Do these Education programs include teaching practice?

Yes, both programs include practicum or teaching-practice components alongside regular theory coursework.

Is LMS and exam file support available for Education students?

Yes, Midterm files, Finalterm files, and LMS handling (quizzes, assignments, GDBs) are available for both Education degree programs.
