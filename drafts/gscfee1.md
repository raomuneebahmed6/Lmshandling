<h2 id="fee-formula">How is the VU summer semester fee calculated?</h2>

The VU summer semester fee is built from credit hours, not from a single fixed price. Three parts make up the total:

- **Tuition fee:** charged per credit hour of every subject you register. Under VU's fee structure effective from Spring 2026, this is **Rs. 1,000 per credit hour** for Associate Degree, Bachelors, BS and BS-Lateral programs. Masters and other programs are charged at their own rates, listed in our [VU fee structure guide](/blog/vu-fee-structure-guide).
- **Enrollment fee:** a per-semester charge. For BS and Associate Degree programs the published figure is Rs. 1,000.
- **Examination fee:** also per semester, published as Rs. 500 for BS and Associate Degree programs.

On top of these, VU lists small recurring charges such as a fee collection charge (around Rs. 100) and a societies, clubs and sports fee (around Rs. 500). Courses with a lab component carry an extra per-credit-hour lab fee.

One honest limit: we could not open VU's official fee page from the tools we use, so these rates come from the fee structure we have already summarised on this site. They match the figures VU students commonly see, but a revision is always possible. Treat them as a guide to the formula, and your voucher as the final word.

<h2 id="worked-example">Worked example: one subject vs two subjects</h2>

Most summer students take one or two subjects. Here is how the formula plays out for a BS student with 3-credit-hour courses, using the rates above:

| Item | 1 subject | 2 subjects |
|---|---|---|
| Tuition (Rs. 1,000 x credit hours) | Rs. 3,000 | Rs. 6,000 |
| Enrollment fee | Rs. 1,000 | Rs. 1,000 |
| Exam fee | Rs. 500 | Rs. 500 |
| Collection charge and sports/societies fee (approx.) | Rs. 600 | Rs. 600 |
| **Estimated total** | **about Rs. 5,100** | **about Rs. 8,100** |

The two-subject total lines up with the roughly Rs. 8,100 figure we mention in our [summer semester registration guide](/blog/vu-summer-semester-registration-guide). The jump from one to two subjects is only the extra tuition (Rs. 3,000), because the per-semester charges are not repeated.

This is an estimate for illustration. A 4-credit course, a lab course, or a different program will change the numbers.

<h2 id="what-is-included">What the fee includes and what it does not</h2>

Paying the voucher confirms your registration in the subjects you selected and covers the semester's exam fee, so you can sit the summer mid and final papers. It also keeps your VULMS access active for those courses.

Things that are **not** covered by this fee:

- The one-time admission, registration and security fees, which you paid when you first joined VU.
- A convocation fee, which is charged only at pass-out.
- Any thesis or research fee for MS and PhD students.
- Tuition for subjects you did not select. Credit hours you do not register are not billed.

Summer is optional. It is mainly useful for repeating an F-grade subject, improving a D, or finishing a remaining course sooner, so count the fee against what you actually need.

<h2 id="how-to-pay">How to pay and when</h2>

The process follows the same pattern each cycle:

1. Log in to VULMS and open the summer semester registration option once VU activates it.
2. Select your subjects from the offered list.
3. Generate the fee voucher (challan) for the selected subjects.
4. Pay it through the bank or payment channel printed on the voucher, and keep the receipt.

We do not list specific bank names or wallet apps here, because the available channels are shown on the voucher itself and can change. The same goes for the deadline: VU announces the summer closing date on VULMS and its notices, and it has been extended before, as in our [deadline extension post](/blog/vu-summer-semester-deadline-extension-2026). Pay as early as you can, since registration is complete only after payment goes through.

<h2 id="confirm-your-fee">How to confirm your own amount</h2>

Before you pay, check three things on your voucher:

- **Subjects and credit hours:** make sure only the courses you meant to register are listed.
- **Program rate:** a Masters or MS student sees different per-credit-hour rates than a BS student.
- **Due date:** the voucher states the last day to pay.

If anything looks wrong, do not pay first and ask later. Contact VU support through the channels on VULMS with a screenshot of the voucher. If you are unsure which subjects suit your plan, the [study scheme](/scheme) shows what each semester normally contains.
