<h2 id="timeline">What is the VU Summer Semester 2026 timeline?</h2>

Searches like "vu summer semester schedule" and "vu summer semester datesheet" usually come from one worry: which date matters next? The table below lists the dates VU published for the 2026 Summer cycle, in order. The dates are specific to this cycle. VU's academic calendar changes every semester.

| Step | Date (2026) | Note |
|---|---|---|
| LMS blocking for unpaid fee | 24 August, and 28 August for Summer | Blocked accounts cannot open VULMS |
| Prototype (third) assignment | 26 August | Needs an unblocked account |
| Mids | 12-13 September | Roughly the first half of the lectures |
| Admission closing | 15 September, extended to 22 September | One extra week |
| Finals | 3-4 October | Comprehensive paper |
| New session classes | 5 October | Starts before results |
| Finalterm paper reschedule | Notice on 6 October, paper on 7 October | Check your own slot |
| Results | 7 October | As scheduled for this cycle |
| Course selection for Summer students | 8 October | Other students opened 18 September |

For the wider calendar around these dates, see our [VU Important Dates 2026](/blog/vu-important-dates-2026) guide.

<h2 id="start-and-registration">When does the VU Summer Semester start and close for registration?</h2>

VU does not announce Summer on a fixed date. Registration shows up inside VULMS (Student Services, then Course Selection) only once VU officially opens it, so there is no calendar date to wait for in advance. For 2026 the admission closing date was 15 September, and VU later moved it to 22 September.

That extension is a good example of why a date copied from last year or from a screenshot is risky. If you are planning a future Summer, watch VULMS and VU's official announcements, and read our [VU Summer Semester registration guide](/blog/vu-summer-semester-registration-guide) for the apply steps and fee side of things.

<h2 id="mids-finals">Mids, Finals and the Summer datesheet</h2>

Summer compresses a full semester into a shorter window, so the gap between Mids and Finals is tighter than in Fall or Spring. In 2026 Mids were on 12-13 September and Finals on 3-4 October, roughly three weeks apart.

The datesheet is not inside VULMS. It lives on VU's separate portal at datesheet.vu.edu.pk. Log in with your usual VULMS Student ID and password, then confirm:

- your exam city and center
- the date and time for each course
- any change since you last checked

Papers can move. VU posted a notice on 6 October rescheduling a Summer Finalterm paper to 7 October, which is why the datesheet entry, not a general calendar, is the date you trust. Our [Summer Finals datesheet post](/blog/vu-summer-semester-finals-datesheet-2026) covers that cycle in detail.

<h2 id="course-selection">VU course selection last date after Summer</h2>

After Summer ends, the next question is course selection. VU opened it on 18 September 2026 for most students (after first announcing 15 September), and on 8 October for students who were enrolled in Summer, once Finals, new session classes and results were underway.

Those are opening dates. The notices this guide relies on did not give a closing date, so a "last date" is something to read on your own course selection screen, because it can differ by student and can be extended. Opening can also happen in stages, so confirm the option is live on your account. The full story is in our [VU course selection dates 2026](/blog/vu-course-selection-dates-2026) guide.

<h2 id="not-published">What to confirm on VULMS yourself</h2>

Some parts of a Summer schedule are set per course, not published as one campus-wide date. Treat these as items to read from your own VULMS rather than numbers to trust from any blog, including this one:

- **Class start for your batch.** The 5 October date above is the new session's start. The first day of Summer lectures depends on when you registered.
- **Quiz and GDB windows.** These open and close per course and are usually short. A blocked account does not pause them, so clear fees before the blocking dates.
- **Assignment due dates.** Each course has its own, shown on its VULMS page.
- **Your exact exam slot.** Take it from the datesheet portal, not a forwarded image.

A short habit helps: once a week, open each course page and note every open and upcoming activity.

It also helps to read the timeline backwards from the exam. Finals on 3-4 October meant new session classes began only a day later, and Results followed on 7 October. A student who also wanted to pick courses for the next semester had Mids prep, Finals prep, new classes and course selection landing within a few weeks of each other. Writing the dates for your own subjects in one place, with the source of each date beside it, makes that stretch much easier to manage and avoids acting on an out-of-date forward.
