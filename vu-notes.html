FAQ

VU exam files, frequently asked questions

What is included in a VU exam file?

Each file is code-wise Objective (MCQs) and Subjective (short and long question) preparation content matched to that course's own lectures, covering the midterm or finalterm portion you choose.

How fast do I receive my file after ordering?

Files are sent directly on WhatsApp after payment is confirmed, usually within minutes during working hours.

Are files available for every VU course code?

Files are available for 606+ VU course codes. If your code isn't showing in search, message Nibaha Haq on WhatsApp to check availability before assuming it's not covered.

Can I get both Midterm and Finalterm files together?

Yes, both are available for most course codes and can be ordered together in the same WhatsApp message.

How do I pay for a file?

Price is confirmed on WhatsApp for your specific code, payment is made through the method shared there, and the file is sent once the payment screenshot is confirmed.
