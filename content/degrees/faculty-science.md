aboutP1: Five programs sit under the Faculty of Science & Technology at Virtual University, and they split fairly cleanly into two groups. Mathematics and Statistics are the quantitative pair (26 and 17 related course codes respectively), built around calculation, proof and data analysis rather than lab work, with enough shared foundation that students often compare the two directly before choosing.
aboutP2: 'Zoology, Bioinformatics and Biotechnology form the life-sciences side, though they differ sharply in size and focus. Zoology, at 38 related course codes, stays closest to classical biology: physiology, ecology, genetics and evolutionary biology taught through diagram- and terminology-heavy coursework. Biotechnology''s 62 related course codes push further into lab-technique and applied-science territory, while Bioinformatics is both the largest program in the faculty and the most computational, with 108 related course codes blending biology with data and computing skills. This puts it closer in spirit to the Mathematics/Statistics pair than to Zoology, despite sitting in the life-sciences group.'
