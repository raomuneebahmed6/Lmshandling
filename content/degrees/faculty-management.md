aboutH2: Faculty of Management
aboutP1: The Faculty of Management at Virtual University groups four programs that share a common foundation but pull in different directions once that foundation is built. Business Administration is by far the largest, with 151 related course codes spanning general management, marketing, HR and operations. It's a broad degree suited to students who want maximum flexibility in the corporate world. Accounting & Finance narrows sharply from there, with 35 related course codes concentrated on financial reporting and analysis, aimed squarely at students planning toward ACCA, ICMA or a CA-track career.
aboutP2: 'Commerce sits between the two in focus but not in size: its 78 related course codes actually put it closer to Business Administration in scale, layering banking and general commerce topics onto the same accounting base Accounting & Finance uses more narrowly. Public Administration is the outlier of the four: instead of preparing students for private-sector business roles, its 66 related course codes point toward government departments, public-sector organizations and NGOs, built around governance and public policy rather than corporate management. All four share early accounting and management foundation courses, which is why switching between them in the first semesters is less disruptive than the differences above might suggest.'

Public Administration is the clear pick only for students specifically targeting government, NGO or CSS/PMS-style careers: its governance and public-policy focus doesn''t transfer as directly to private-sector roles as the other three.'
